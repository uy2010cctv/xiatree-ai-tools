Privacy

Effective 27 November 2026. Speaking Studio is a static, local-first practice tool.

What stays on your device

Theme preference, a verified-access marker and up to 200 self-assessments are stored in this browser’s localStorage. Notes and scores are not sent to the seller. Microphone audio stays in this tab’s memory. It is not uploaded or automatically saved. Reloading or closing the tab discards takes, so download recordings you want to keep.

Microphone and speech

Recording starts only after you enable your microphone and start a speaking take. A volume visualization uses local Web Audio processing. We select only speech voices reported by the browser as local Mandarin voices. If none are ready, the app shows timed source text instead. The browser or operating system controls voice availability and audio permissions.

When a connection is used

Initial loading contacts the site host. First activation and any manual verification send your license key and the public product ID directly to Gumroad. Gumroad sees the normal network information associated with that request. We discard the entered key after successful activation; we do not retain the key, purchase email or full API response in localStorage. Checkout and digital delivery occur on Gumroad under its own policies. Opening official resource links contacts those sites. There are no analytics, advertising scripts, external fonts or third-party CDN assets.

Offline copy and deletion

On supported HTTPS or localhost installations, a service worker saves the app files locally after first loading. A downloaded complete webapp folder also works locally, subject to browser media restrictions. Clear history removes stored self-ratings; deactivate removes the access marker. To remove theme and offline assets too, clear this site’s browser storage or delete the downloaded folder. Clearing site data requires activation again. Export history first if you want a backup.

Contact

For a paid-order question, use the seller contact shown on your Gumroad receipt. Do not send private recordings unless you explicitly choose to share them.
