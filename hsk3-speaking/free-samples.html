Level 3 · Repeat · 0s prep / 10s response

A change of plan

Prompt

今天下午我要先去银行，再去超市买水果。

Jīntiān xiàwǔ wǒ yào xiān qù yínháng, zài qù chāoshì mǎi shuǐguǒ.

This afternoon I will go to the bank first, then buy fruit at the supermarket.

Build your response

Track who, what changed, why, and the result. Repeat exactly at level 3; retell in your own words at levels 4–6.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

今天下午我要先去银行，再去超市买水果。

This afternoon I will go to the bank first, then buy fruit at the supermarket.

Why it works

Keep the order of the two errands. Pause after 银行, without dropping the second 去. The task is repetition, so adding a reason would change the source.

Common pitfalls

Replacing source facts with a personal opinion. Losing the cause, sequence or final outcome.

Useful language & follow-up

银行 (yínháng): bank; 超市 (chāoshì): supermarket

你一般什么时候买东西？

A morning routine

Prompt

每天早上，我先喝一杯水，然后走路去学校。路上我会听中文。

Měitiān zǎoshang, wǒ xiān hē yì bēi shuǐ, ránhòu zǒulù qù xuéxiào. Lùshang wǒ huì tīng Zhōngwén.

Every morning I drink a glass of water, then walk to school. I listen to Chinese on the way.

Build your response

Mark phrase boundaries. Keep the text intact; pause at punctuation.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

每天早上，我先喝一杯水，然后走路去学校。路上我会听中文。

Every morning I drink a glass of water, then walk to school. I listen to Chinese on the way.

Why it works

Read in thought groups: time, first action, next action. 一杯 is normally yì bēi in connected speech. Do not give every character equal stress.

Common pitfalls

Reading character by character instead of in meaningful phrases. Changing a word or skipping a clause to keep up.

Useful language & follow-up

然后 (ránhòu): then; 走路 (zǒulù): walk

你早上做的第一件事是什么？

Level 3 · Picture · 45s prep / 15s response

The shared table

Prompt

请描述咖啡馆里两个人在做什么。

Qǐng miáoshù kāfēiguǎn lǐ liǎng ge rén zài zuò shénme.

Describe what the two people in the café are doing.

Build your response

Place → people → actions → cautious inference → relevant comment.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

两个人坐在咖啡馆里。左边的人正在看电脑，右边的人拿着一本书。桌上有两杯咖啡。他们可能在一起学习。我也喜欢在安静的地方看书。

Two people are sitting in a café. The person on the left is looking at a laptop, and the one on the right is holding a book. There are two coffees on the table. They may be studying together. I also like reading in quiet places.

Why it works

Start with people and place. Use 正在 for an action and 有 for an object. 可能 marks a sensible guess; do not state an invented relationship as visible fact.

Common pitfalls

Presenting an imagined relationship as a visible fact. Listing objects without describing actions or linking ideas.

Useful language & follow-up

左边 (zuǒbian): on the left; 安静 (ānjìng): quiet

你喜欢在哪里学习？

Level 4 · Retell · 0s prep / 40s response

A missed delivery

Prompt

小林买的书今天到了，可是他正在上课。快递员把书放在门口，给他发了消息。他下课后马上把书取回了家。

Xiǎo Lín mǎi de shū jīntiān dào le, kěshì tā zhèngzài shàngkè. Kuàidìyuán bǎ shū fàng zài ménkǒu, gěi tā fā le xiāoxi. Tā xiàkè hòu mǎshàng bǎ shū qǔ huí le jiā.

Retell the story: a book delivery arrives while Xiao Lin is in class; the courier leaves it at the door and sends a message; he collects it after class.

Build your response

Track who, what changed, why, and the result. Repeat exactly at level 3; retell in your own words at levels 4–6.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

小林的书今天送到了，当时他在上课。快递员把书放在门口，并发消息通知他。下课以后，他就把书拿回家了。

Xiao Lin's books arrived while he was in class. The courier left them at the door and sent him a message. After class he took them home.

Why it works

This is retelling, not verbatim repetition. Preserve arrival, temporary absence, notification and collection. 取回 can become 拿回 without losing the event.

Common pitfalls

Replacing source facts with a personal opinion. Losing the cause, sequence or final outcome.

Useful language & follow-up

快递员 (kuàidìyuán): courier; 通知 (tōngzhī): notify

如果包裹丢了，应该怎么办？

Level 4 · Picture · 45s prep / 120s response

Studying together

Prompt

请描述咖啡馆里的学习情景，并说明合作可能有什么帮助。

Qǐng miáoshù kāfēiguǎn lǐ de xuéxí qíngjǐng, bìng shuōmíng hézuò kěnéng yǒu shénme bāngzhù.

Describe the café study scene and explain how cooperation could help.

Build your response

Place → people → actions → cautious inference → relevant comment.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

图中两个人面对面坐着，一个使用电脑，另一个拿着书。桌上放着咖啡，环境看起来比较安静。他们可能在为同一个任务查资料。如果是在合作学习，一个人可以负责查找，另一个人整理重点。不过，图片本身不能告诉我们他们的关系，所以不能确定他们是不是同学。

Two people sit facing each other, one with a laptop and one with a book. The café looks quiet. They might be researching a shared task: one could search and the other organize key points. Their relationship cannot be established from the picture alone.

Why it works

Move from visible evidence to a clearly signposted possibility. Use 一个…另一个… to organize people. Avoid describing a relationship as if the illustration proves it.

Common pitfalls

Presenting an imagined relationship as a visible fact. Listing objects without describing actions or linking ideas.

Useful language & follow-up

合作 (hézuò): cooperate; 资料 (zīliào): reference material

Level 4 · Topic · 45s prep / 120s response

A mistake that helped

Prompt

请介绍一次错误，以及你从中学到了什么。

Qǐng jièshào yí cì cuòwù, yǐjí nǐ cóng zhōng xué dào le shénme.

Describe a mistake and what you learned from it.

Build your response

Position → reason → concrete example → qualification → closing point.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

有一次，我把小组作业的截止时间记错了，以为星期五才交，实际上星期四就到期了。我发现以后先向同学说明情况，再和大家分配剩下的任务。虽然最后按时交了，内容却不够仔细。从那以后，我会把重要日期写进日历，并提前一天检查进度。对我来说，承认错误只是第一步，改变做事的方法才更重要。

I once wrote down the wrong deadline for a group assignment. When I noticed, I told my classmates and we divided the remaining work. We submitted on time, but the result lacked care. Now I put deadlines in a calendar and check progress a day early. Admitting a mistake is only the first step; changing the process matters more.

Why it works

Use situation, action, consequence and lesson. Keeping the imperfect result makes the story credible. 截止时间 is the deadline, while 到期 describes it being reached.

Common pitfalls

Delaying the answer with a memorized introduction. Giving claims without a specific example or qualifying condition.

Useful language & follow-up

截止时间 (jiézhǐ shíjiān): deadline; 分配 (fēnpèi): allocate

如果队友犯了同样的错误呢？

Level 5 · Retell · 0s prep / 90s response

A library experiment

Prompt

一家图书馆把闭馆时间从六点延长到九点，原本担心费用会大幅增加。试行一个月后，晚间读者明显增多，主要是下班后的年轻人。图书馆通过调整排班控制了成本，决定继续开放夜间自习区。

Yì jiā túshūguǎn bǎ bìguǎn shíjiān cóng liù diǎn yáncháng dào jiǔ diǎn, yuánběn dānxīn fèiyòng huì dàfú zēngjiā. Shìxíng yí ge yuè hòu, wǎnjiān dúzhě míngxiǎn zēngduō, zhǔyào shì xiàbān hòu de niánqīngrén. Túshūguǎn tōngguò tiáozhěng páibān kòngzhì le chéngběn, juédìng jìxù kāifàng yèjiān zìxíqū.

Retell the library's extended-hours trial, its results and its decision.

Build your response

Track who, what changed, why, and the result. Repeat exactly at level 3; retell in your own words at levels 4–6.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

一家图书馆尝试把闭馆时间推迟三个小时。虽然最初担心成本上涨，但一个月的试行表明，晚间需求确实很大，尤其是下班后的年轻人。通过调整员工排班，图书馆控制了额外支出，因此决定保留夜间自习区。

A library tried closing three hours later. It initially feared higher costs, but a month-long trial showed strong evening demand, especially from young workers. Staff scheduling kept extra spending under control, so the evening study area was retained.

Why it works

Retain the concern, evidence and decision. Three hours later is an accurate compression of six to nine. Do not claim that all library services remain open at night.

Common pitfalls

Replacing source facts with a personal opinion. Losing the cause, sequence or final outcome.

Useful language & follow-up

延长 (yáncháng): extend; 成本 (chéngběn): cost

如何判断这项服务是否值得保留？

Level 5 · Read · 30s prep / 60s response

Repairing trust

Prompt

真正有效的道歉，不只是表达后悔。受到影响的人通常更关心问题会怎样解决，以及同样的事情是否还会发生。因此，承认责任、提出具体的补救办法，并在之后兑现承诺，往往比反复解释自己的动机更重要。

Zhēnzhèng yǒuxiào de dàoqiàn, bù zhǐ shì biǎodá hòuhuǐ. Shòudào yǐngxiǎng de rén tōngcháng gèng guānxīn wèntí huì zěnyàng jiějué, yǐjí tóngyàng de shìqing shìfǒu hái huì fāshēng. Yīncǐ, chéngrèn zérèn, tíchū jùtǐ de bǔjiù bànfǎ, bìng zài zhīhòu duìxiàn chéngnuò, wǎngwǎng bǐ fǎnfù jiěshì zìjǐ de dòngjī gèng zhòngyào.

An effective apology involves responsibility, concrete remedies and keeping promises, not just regret or explanations.

Build your response

Mark phrase boundaries. Keep the text intact; pause at punctuation.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

真正有效的道歉，不只是表达后悔。受到影响的人通常更关心问题会怎样解决，以及同样的事情是否还会发生。因此，承认责任、提出具体的补救办法，并在之后兑现承诺，往往比反复解释自己的动机更重要。

An effective apology is more than an expression of regret. Those affected want to know how the problem will be resolved and whether it will happen again. Accepting responsibility, proposing remedies and keeping promises matter more than repeatedly explaining motives.

Why it works

Pause before the three parallel actions. Hold the comparison open until 更重要. Avoid turning every comma into a full stop, which obscures the argument.

Common pitfalls

Reading character by character instead of in meaningful phrases. Changing a word or skipping a clause to keep up.

Useful language & follow-up

补救 (bǔjiù): remedy; 承诺 (chéngnuò): promise

怎样的道歉让你觉得有诚意？

Level 5 · Topic · 45s prep / 150s response

A four-day workweek

Prompt

工作天数减少一定能提高生活质量吗？

Gōngzuò tiānshù jiǎnshǎo yídìng néng tígāo shēnghuó zhìliàng ma?

Does reducing the number of working days necessarily improve quality of life?

Build your response

Position → reason → concrete example → qualification → closing point.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

我认为不一定，关键在于工作量是否也得到合理调整。如果只是把五天的任务压缩到四天，员工可能每天更累，甚至把工作带回家。相反，如果企业减少无效会议、明确任务重点，较短的工作周就可能带来真正的休息。以服务行业为例，还必须考虑顾客需求和轮班安排。因此，与其只统计上班天数，不如同时观察总工时、工作压力和收入是否稳定。这样才能判断改革有没有改善生活。

Not necessarily: the workload must change too. Compressing five days of tasks into four may increase exhaustion or unpaid work at home. Reducing unnecessary meetings and setting priorities could create real rest, but service businesses also need coverage. I would assess total hours, stress and income stability rather than count days alone.

Why it works

Challenge 一定 with a mechanism and counterexample. The final indicators turn a vague preference into an assessable position. Do not claim a study or statistic you cannot name.

Common pitfalls

Delaying the answer with a memorized introduction. Giving claims without a specific example or qualifying condition.

Useful language & follow-up

压缩 (yāsuō): compress; 工时 (gōngshí): working hours

如果工资也减少，你还支持吗？

Level 6 · Read · 30s prep / 60s response

Evidence and confidence

Prompt

信息传播得越快，判断就越需要耐心。一段表达流畅、立场鲜明的文字，未必拥有可靠的证据。面对复杂问题，我们既要区分事实与推断，也要说明结论适用的条件。承认暂时不知道，并不是放弃判断，而是为之后修正认识保留空间。

Xìnxī chuánbō de yuè kuài, pànduàn jiù yuè xūyào nàixīn. Yí duàn biǎodá liúchàng, lìchǎng xiānmíng de wénzì, wèibì yōngyǒu kěkào de zhèngjù. Miànduì fùzá wèntí, wǒmen jì yào qūfēn shìshí yǔ tuīduàn, yě yào shuōmíng jiélùn shìyòng de tiáojiàn. Chéngrèn zànshí bù zhīdào, bìng bú shì fàngqì pànduàn, ér shì wèi zhīhòu xiūzhèng rènshi bǎoliú kōngjiān.

Faster information calls for more patient judgment. Fluent assertions are not evidence; distinguishing facts, inferences and conditions leaves room for correction.

Build your response

Mark phrase boundaries. Keep the text intact; pause at punctuation.

Exemplary answer · editorial 9/9

信息传播得越快，判断就越需要耐心。一段表达流畅、立场鲜明的文字，未必拥有可靠的证据。面对复杂问题，我们既要区分事实与推断，也要说明结论适用的条件。承认暂时不知道，并不是放弃判断，而是为之后修正认识保留空间。

As information spreads faster, judgment needs more patience. Fluent, confident prose may lack evidence. We need to distinguish facts from inferences and state the conditions under which conclusions apply. Admitting uncertainty leaves room to revise understanding.

Why it works

Make the paired structures audible: 越…越…, 既…也…, 并不是…而是…. Stress conceptual contrasts, not every abstract noun. Let the final phrase resolve the paragraph.

Common pitfalls

Reading character by character instead of in meaningful phrases. Changing a word or skipping a clause to keep up.

Useful language & follow-up

推断 (tuīduàn): inference; 适用 (shìyòng): apply to

你会在什么情况下改变自己的观点？
