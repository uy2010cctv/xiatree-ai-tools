Privacy Policy & GDPR Notice — EuroEase for Shopify

Last updated: November 23, 2026

EuroEase is operated by Xiatree Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"). We process Shopify merchant and order data strictly to generate legally required EU VAT invoices, Credit Notes, Peppol BIS 3.0 UBL XML files, and optional EU GPSR product safety metafields.

1. Data Collected via Shopify APIs & Webhooks

  • Order & Billing Data (orders/paid, orders/cancelled, refunds/create): line items, tax rates, currency, billing name/company/address, and buyer VAT ID (if provided) solely to generate immutable fiscal documents.
  • Product Metafields (optional GPSR feature): manufacturer and EU Responsible Person safety information written to native Shopify product metafields (euroease_gpsr).
  • We do not inject storefront scripts, do not track shopper browsing behavior, and never sell or share merchant or customer data with third-party advertisers.

2. Statutory Tax Retention & GDPR Requests

EuroEase implements mandatory Shopify GDPR webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, shop/redact). Where European statutory tax retention laws (such as German GoBD / AO § 147) require retaining fiscal invoices, non-essential personal identifiers are minimized upon request while preserving legally mandated accounting integrity.

3. Third-Party Sub-Processors

  • European Commission VIES REST API: receives only the EU country code and VAT number to verify B2B reverse-charge eligibility.
  • Shopify Billing API: manages all subscription charges securely inside Shopify Admin.

4. Contact & Data Protection

For GDPR data access, export, or deletion inquiries, contact support@toolstoken.com.
