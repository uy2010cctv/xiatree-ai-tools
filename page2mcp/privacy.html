Privacy Policy — Page2MCP

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Page2MCP is operated by Xiatree Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. We follow a strict local-first, minimal-data architecture.

Data Handling Principles

  • All DOM parsing and Markdown conversion runs 100% locally inside your Chrome browser.
  • Page2MCP never uploads browsing history or page contents to external third-party servers.
  • Optional Local MCP Bridge sync only sends the exact page you manually click 'Push' on to your configured localhost endpoint.

Payment Processing

All payments and global tax compliance (VAT/GST/Sales Tax) are handled by our Merchant of Record payment partner (Paddle / Creem / Lemon Squeezy / Gumroad). We never store credit card numbers.
